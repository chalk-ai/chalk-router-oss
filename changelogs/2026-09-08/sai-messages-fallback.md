- `/v1/messages` now falls back to a configured alternate model when the primary connection's request fails, for both streaming and non-streaming clients.
- That fallback now also covers native and adapted Anthropic Messages upstreams, not just OpenAI-translated ones: a 429 or 5xx native/adapted response now retries the next configured alternate the same way a translated failure already did. Any other non-2xx status (e.g. 400) is still forwarded to the client unchanged with no retry, and if every candidate fails, the final upstream response's status, headers, and body are forwarded byte-for-byte rather than replaced with a synthesized error.
